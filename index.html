Rhinegold! let the blessèd sun shine upon this paradise: bushels o’ wheat and hay. divine, gracious lips soft eyes gleam and glimmer just so. ah! how a face, sublime, basks in such sunlight: by Jove, what a marvel! be as chaste as Adam in his first breath with his fair maiden’s glistening cheeks all aglow. be as gentle as you are kind. be as fervent as the most passionate lover: loins bound in eternal troth. honeyed whiskey and wine soaks and trickles like a sap. the idyllic sunshine gleams o’er the golden fields. ay, the gold burns hot and scorches! i pray something wicked this way comes. your touch is as holy as a pilgrim’s faithful kiss. and, yet, it is your sinful gaze that i desire. there you are, Jove! eyes smoldering and all ablaze. all that glitters is shining, beguiled gold. there is a lustrous heat that burns mine eyes Rhinegold!
